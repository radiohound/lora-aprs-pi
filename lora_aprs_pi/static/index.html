        Beacon
        
        
        
        
    
          
          
        
    
        
        
        
        
        
        
        
    Lowers the beacon power, never above the power set under Radio, while a digipeater
          reports hearing you with plenty to spare. Goes back up when the margin gets thin or no report comes back.
          Needs Experimental LACF beacons (or alternate) and an Experimental LACF digipeater, since only Experimental LACF repeats carry the report.
    
      
    

      
    
        Digipeater
        
        
    Packets addressed to this station's callsign are repeated too. Experimental LACF packets are repeated as Experimental LACF, with this station's signal report added.
    
      
    

      
    
        iGate
        
        
    For licensed amateurs. The station also puts its own position on APRS-IS, without using air time.
    
        
    
          
          
        
    
        
        
    
      
    

      
    
        MQTT (map and network diagnostics)
        
        
    The details are already filled in. Tick the box and save. What this station hears, with its signal
          strength, plus a status line each minute, goes to the
          tiles.aivu.com/lora map, and lets the
          developers see how the network is doing and where problems are. Never your settings, passwords or WiFi details,
          and never packets with RFONLY or NOGATE in the path. The connection is not encrypted and the login is shared and public.
